## General Idea

A guessing game where based on limited information you try to guess the current randomly selected thing (like an item or a character) from something (like a game).

## For

For people who want to test their knowledge about something.

## Services

Will use database to keep info about things to guess, and accounts where player's have their best winstreak kept.
